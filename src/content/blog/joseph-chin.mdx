When GPT-3 came out, Joseph Chin was a software engineer in the UK. He thought AI was about to end his career, so he quit and moved back to Malaysia to be near family and build.

He built an app that has over 160,000 users, and Iman Gadzhi featured it. He started AI Tinkerers in Kuala Lumpur. He co-founded Humeo, the San Francisco company behind VideoClaw, an AI agent that edits video, and he created DocuAsk.

Ashvin and Ken talk with him about what is left for a person when a model writes the code, the habit that let a non-technical friend ship real apps, and why AI still cannot really watch a video.
